A Legacy of Musical Excellence

Simon Joseph is more than just a musician; he is an institution in music education. With a profound understanding of harmonic theory, rhythm, and instrumental mechanics, he has dedicated his life to the pursuit of musical perfection. His expertise bridges the gap between classical foundational techniques and modern contemporary styles, making his approach incredibly versatile and highly sought after.

As a highly respected educator at Accord School, Sir Simon has profoundly impacted the school's cultural landscape. He is responsible for nurturing the raw talent of countless students, guiding them from their very first hesitant notes to performing confidently on grand stages. At Accord School, his music room is not just a classroom—it is a sanctuary for creativity, discipline, and artistic growth.

Teaching Philosophy & Vision

Sir's teaching philosophy is rooted in the powerful belief that every student possesses a unique musical voice waiting to be discovered. He does not believe in a "one-size-fits-all" approach. Instead, whether a student is picking up the acoustic guitar to learn chords, shredding on an electric guitar, or exploring complex sonatas on the keyboard, he meticulously tailors his lesson plans to fit the individual's pace and ambition.

Beyond technical proficiency, Simon Joseph emphasizes ear training, improvisational skills, and stage presence. His students at Accord School are routinely prepared for school assemblies, high-stakes talent shows, and comprehensive musical grading exams, ensuring they are not just playing an instrument, but truly understanding the language of music.

Curriculum & Expertise

Master the fundamentals. Command the stage.

Guitar Mastery

Comprehensive training spanning Acoustic, Classical, and Electric guitar. From foundational open chords and rhythmic strumming to advanced fretboard navigation, fingerstyle techniques, and complex rock/metal solos.

Keyboard & Piano

Deep-dive instruction into piano mechanics. Students learn sight-reading, scale mastery, chord progressions, and independent hand coordination, bridging classical discipline with modern pop and jazz elements.

Accord School Support

Exclusive mentorship for Accord School students. Dedicated preparation for school events, practical grading exams, band formations, and live performance coaching to build unbreakable stage confidence.
